# Code of Conduct

OneCLIQ is an open-source project. People should be able to report a bug, ask a question, or review code without being treated badly. This applies to newcomers and long-time contributors alike.

## Our expectations

When you take part in project discussions or contributions:

- Be respectful, even when you disagree. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different levels of experience and explain technical terms when they matter.
- Give useful, specific feedback and make room for others to respond.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.
- Assume good intent when possible, but take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, stalking, doxxing, and persistent personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not publish someone else's private information or use project spaces to intimidate others.

## Where this applies

These rules apply in OneCLIQ issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed for the project. They also apply when someone publicly represents the project, such as speaking on its behalf at an event.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness misconduct, contact a repository maintainer through a private channel they have made available on their GitHub profile or another official project page. If no private contact channel is listed, open a GitHub issue that asks for a private way to contact maintainers. Do not include sensitive personal details in that public issue.

Describe what happened and include links or dates if it is safe to do so. You can report behavior that affects you or someone else. Maintainers should handle reports with care, share details only as needed to investigate, and consider the safety and wishes of the people affected. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on third-party services, so avoid sending more personal information than necessary.

## How maintainers may respond

Maintainers review reports in context and may ask for more information. Depending on the behavior and its impact, they may explain the expectations, request an apology or change in conduct, remove content, limit participation, or ban someone from project spaces. Serious threats or harassment may lead to immediate action.

People involved in a report should not decide its outcome if they have a conflict of interest. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code of conduct is intended to keep collaboration safe and useful. It does not replace platform rules or applicable law.
